Basically makes it easy to create professional cloud architecture diagrams right in your browser. No sign-up required — just open the canvas and start building.

## Creating Your First Diagram

1. **Open the canvas** — Head to the homepage and you'll see an empty canvas ready to go.
2. **Add nodes** — Use the sidebar to drag cloud service icons onto the canvas. We support AWS, Azure, and GCP services.
3. **Connect nodes** — Click and drag from one node's handle to another to create edges representing data flow or dependencies.
4. **Customize** — Double-click any node or edge to edit labels, colors, and styles.

## Tips for Better Diagrams

- **Keep it simple** — Focus on the key components and their relationships. You can always add detail later.
- **Use grouping** — Group related services together to make your diagram easier to read.
- **Export when ready** — Export your diagram as PNG or GIF to share with your team.

Happy diagramming!
